# MBIS in the charge engine: converged fit, fitted guess, actinides

Date: 2026-10-15. Branch feat/mbis-heavy-elements. Code: `core/charge_engine.py`
(`mbis_fit`, `mbis_select`, `mbis_fitted_shells`, `mbis_objective`), table
`data/mbis_free_atom.py` (generated by `scripts/fit_mbis_free_atom_table.py`).

## Summary

- Multiwfn's MBIS stops when no charge moves by 1e-4 e in a cycle (500 cycles max). On
  30 local molecules that left 4 more than 0.03 e from the fixed point (up to 0.25 e),
  and on actinides 0.03-0.38 e. The engine now converges to the fixed point by default
  (`MBIS_MODE = "converged"`); `--mbis_mode multiwfn` keeps Multiwfn's rule for the
  parity tests.
- MBIS can have several fixed points. The engine fits from three guesses and keeps the
  one with the smallest Kullback-Leibler divergence; `_meta` records the choice.
- The guess is a free-atom MBIS table fitted to the engine's own Hirshfeld proatoms
  (Z 1-103). It is 4.4x faster than Multiwfn's guess and finds equal or better fits.
- MBIS now runs for Z 87-118 (6 shells) and for ECP atoms with an EDF core density,
  which `mbis_ecp=False` / `--no_mbis_ecp` turns off.

## What the fit does

1. **Guess.** `mbis_fitted_shells`: per element, the spherical MBIS fit of its free-atom
   density (`multiwfn_atmraddens`, the Hirshfeld proatoms). The shell count follows
   Multiwfn's period rule; period 7 keeps 6 shells, because a seventh merges with a
   neighbour (denspart's own fits merge 7 into 6 shells for Z 89-114). No shell merged or
   emptied in our fits. Compared with denspart's table (GPL, not shipped; used only as a
   check), 99 of 103 elements have the same shell count; populations differ by a median
   0.5 e and widths by 8%, as expected from different free-atom densities.
2. **Iteration** (`_mbis_converged`). Anderson acceleration (history 6) on the log
   populations and log widths, with a trust factor of 2 against the plain step.
   - Converged: no population moves by 1e-8 e and no width by 1e-8 relative in one plain
     step.
   - Fallback to plain iteration from the same guess when acceleration does not converge
     in 1000 evaluations, or ends on a degenerate point (`mbis_degenerate`: two shells of
     an atom within 0.1% in width, or a shell below 1e-6 e). Unguarded acceleration
     reached such points that plain iteration leaves (Li 1s emptied, two shells merged).
3. **Selection** (`mbis_select`, on the MBIS charge grid). Fits from the fitted guess and
   from it with every width scaled by 0.6 and 1.5. It keeps a non-degenerate fit with the
   largest `mbis_objective`: the sum of w ln rho0 over the grid, which is the part of
   int rho ln(rho / rho0) that differs between fixed points. Ties within 1e-12 relative go
   to the earlier guess. The fuzzy refit on the main grid is a single fit from the
   winning guess.
4. **`_meta` fields.**
   - `mbis_mode`, `mbis_cycles`;
   - `mbis_selection`: the selected guess, its objective, degenerate flags, the spread
     and the number of distinct solutions;
   - `mbis_unique`: one solution within 0.005 e;
   - `mbis_degenerate`;
   - `mbis_beyond_multiwfn`: Z > 86;
   - `skipped`, with the reason `ecp_disabled` when `mbis_ecp=False`.

## How it behaves (validation sets)

Molecules: 27 from `data/cross_validation_wfns` (periods 2-6, 2-33 atoms, the smallest
per period), 5 actinide nitrates (Th, U, Np, Pu x2; X2C and ECP), and Fe2, Zn2+ and
Cs+ clusters (10-39 atoms). Times are the charge-grid selection, at 16 threads.

| check | result |
|---|---|
| converged fit vs plain iteration far past Multiwfn's rule, same guess | 2.5e-6 e or less on non-stalling jobs |
| Multiwfn's rule vs converged, Z <= 86 (27 jobs) | 3 jobs off by more than 0.03 e, 7 by more than 0.005 e |
| Multiwfn's rule vs converged, actinides (5 jobs) | 0.03-0.38 e |
| fitted-guess selection vs Multiwfn-guess selection (35 jobs) | 32 the same within 0.005 e; in the 3 that differ (O13 0.023 e, Np25 0.19 e, Zn20 0.37 e) the fitted guess has the better objective |
| selection when every guess has 20% of its core population moved to the valence shell | 34 of 35 the same within 0.005 e; Np25 lands on the worse solution |
| more than one solution (fitted guess) | 3 of 35: Cl6, Pu10 (X2C-SVPall), Np25 |
| time, fitted vs Multiwfn guess, all 35 jobs | 259 s vs 1132 s |

Cost per molecule (charge-grid selection, fitted guess):

| molecule | time |
|---|---|
| organic or main-group, 4-274 atoms | about 1.7x one Multiwfn-rule fit (converged fits take about 40 cycles) |
| Fe2 complex, 10 atoms | 6 s |
| Zn2+ cluster, 20 atoms | 12 s |
| Cs+ cluster, 39 atoms | 32 s |
| actinides, 10-25 atoms | 5-57 s |

The cycle count depends on the elements, not the molecule size. The cost per cycle
grows about linearly with the number of atoms.

## ECP atoms

MBIS is defined for all-electron densities (Verstraelen et al., JCTC 2016). For ECP atoms
the engine adds Multiwfn's EDF core density, a reconstructed all-electron density. It
runs MBIS on that by default; `mbis_ecp=False` skips the MBIS family for any record with
ECP atoms and records why. An ECP atom without an EDF is always skipped.

## Literature context

The literature review was done in the 2026-10-14 session.

- **Non-uniqueness is documented.** The original paper states that multiple stationary
  points may exist and that a reasonable guess is needed. Cheng et al., JCP 2025, mark
  MBIS as not unique.
- **No published code or paper uses multi-start with lowest-divergence selection.** It is
  new method and should be disclosed with the data.
- **Stopping rules in other codes** are tighter than Multiwfn's 1e-4 e:
  - 1e-8 on the density change: the paper, Psi4, Cheng;
  - 1e-6 on charges: ORCA, horton-part.
- **Element range in other codes:**
  - Psi4 refuses Z > 36;
  - Multiwfn refuses Z > 86;
  - only denspart and horton-part define guesses up to Z = 118.
- **Actinides:** no published MBIS validation was found.
- **SPICE** stores MBIS and drops it where it fails to converge, without a failure rate.

## Caveats and open points

- Actinide MBIS has no reference: it rests on convergence, the divergence selection and
  the checks above.
- Grid convergence for Z > 86 is still open. The 70 radial points of the MBIS grid moved
  Pu X2C-SVPall charges by up to 0.12 e against 120 points in an earlier check, at the
  Multiwfn guess.
- A permanent skip (`ecp_disabled`, `ecp_without_edf`, `z_above_118`) still leaves the
  record without a `charge_engine` timing, so the runner retries it. A terminal state for
  permanent skips is still to be designed.
- Stored MBIS values (Multiwfn's rule) differ from converged ones by the amounts above.
  They are not mixed with converged data within a dataset.
